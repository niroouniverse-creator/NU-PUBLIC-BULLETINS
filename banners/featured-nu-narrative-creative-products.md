# Featured Notice — N.U Narrative & Creative Product Research

**N.U Storytelling · Worldbuilding · Creative Products · Stationery**  
**Status:** Research/proposal notice — not a product launch, Canon amendment, paid opportunity, or rights grant.

Niroo Universe (N.U) is organizing a research and development direction to make its stories, worlds, and creative identity easier to explore and build upon.

The research connects:
- Storytelling, character development, narrative voice, and worldbuilding.
- Visual and sensory storytelling across different media.
- Creative products such as journals, notebooks, sketchbooks, prompt cards, bookmarks, stickers, and writing instruments.
- Practical entry paths for writers, illustrators, designers, product makers, members, and interested creators.

**Purpose:** help people discover the N.U creative ecosystem and understand possible ways to develop stories and useful products while respecting source continuity, attribution, and rights.

**Current status:** research and classification / proposal under review. This is not a product launch, open paid opportunity, Canon amendment, licensing grant, or confirmation that any listed product is available.

**فارسی:** پژوهش و دسته‌بندی مسیر توسعهٔ روایت‌ها، جهان‌سازی، آثار خلاقانه و محصولات کاربردی و نوشت‌افزار N.U برای اعضا و علاقه‌مندان. این اعلان معرفی یک مسیر پژوهشی است، نه اعلام عرضهٔ محصول یا تأیید رسمی Canon.
