# ANN-2026-0004 — N.U Quarantine, Archive Identity and Conflict Control
**Published:** 2026-11-09  
**Status:** PUBLIC — MODEL-READABLE DEVELOPMENT STANDARD

N.U preserves useful ideas and source records without automatically approving or executing them. Every official archive record needs a stable identifier, a clear reason for retention, its current status, and a route for retrieval.

## Read the protocol
[N.U Quarantine, Archive Identity and Conflict-Control Protocol](https://github.com/niroouniverse-creator/N.U-Protocol-Registry/blob/main/protocols/NU-QUARANTINE-AND-CONFLICT-CONTROL.md)

## Core rules
- Quarantine is a holding and access-control state, not a rejection.
- Similar records may be linked or consolidated only when their meaning, scope, and time context are compatible; original records remain preserved.
- Material contradictions receive their own case ID and remain unresolved until an authorized decision is documented.
- Deferred ideas keep a stable ID, archive reason, feasibility, dependencies, and next decision.
- An archived idea is not automatically approved for execution.
- Models without private archive access must not claim to have registered or quarantined an item in the private system.
