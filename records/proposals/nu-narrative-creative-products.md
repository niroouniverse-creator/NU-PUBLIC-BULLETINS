# N.U Narrative & Creative Product Development — Research Notice

- Listing ID: nu-narrative-creative-products
- Type: proposal
- Status: UNDER_REVIEW
- Created: 2026-11-08
- Public scope: discovery metadata and a non-confidential summary only.

## Summary

N.U is organizing a research direction that connects its existing narrative and worldbuilding materials with practical creator guidance and possible creative-product development. The purpose is to make it easier for members and interested creators to explore storytelling, characters, settings, visual language, and useful products such as journals, notebooks, sketchbooks, prompt cards, bookmarks, stickers, and writing instruments.

## Research areas

1. Existing N.U stories, works, and narrative modes.
2. Character, voice, setting, atmosphere, and continuity analysis.
3. Worldbuilding and source-based Canon boundaries.
4. Visual and sensory storytelling across media.
5. Narrative layers for functional products and stationery.
6. Creator onboarding, attribution, community participation, and rights awareness.

## Current status and limitations

This is a research notice and development proposal, not an approved implementation plan. The narrative taxonomy is provisional. Product families are concepts for evaluation, not approved SKUs or manufacturing specifications. No product availability, paid opportunity, licensing permission, Canon change, or open submission process is announced.

Detailed working files and unreviewed material remain in private repositories. Only the minimum public discovery information is listed here.
