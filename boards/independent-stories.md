## Recognition principle
An independent work does not need to become an official N.U reference to have value. If the creator publishes it independently and wants it discoverable, it may be listed here after the public URL, attribution, privacy, and rights notes are checked.

An independent work remains independent as a work unless a separate authorized decision accepts it as an N.U reference or official N.U production. If it is published/listed on a connected official N.U public network surface, that inclusion is team-approved for network inclusion only; it does not automatically change the work's Canon status, ownership, licensing or rights.


## Official network inclusion and independent status

Publication/listing on a connected official N.U public network surface is team-approved for network inclusion. This does not convert an independent work into core N.U Canon, transfer ownership, grant reproduction/adaptation rights, or automatically make it an official N.U production. Keep the work's actual relationship label and creator attribution visible.

A work that has not been published or listed on the connected official network, and has no separate authorized N.U decision, must not be represented as an official network item. It may still be acknowledged here as an independent work if the creator wants it listed and the URL, attribution, privacy and rights checks pass.

## Listing requirements
- Stable independent-work ID.
- Exact title, creator credit, language, medium, and version/date where known.
- Original public URL.
- Short neutral description and useful keywords.
- Relationship label: independent interpretation, fan work, community research, commentary, or other.
- Rights/permission state where relevant.
- Listing status and last-checked date.
- Link to the [Creator Review and Recognition Protocol](https://github.com/niroouniverse-creator/N.U-Protocol-Registry/blob/main/protocols/NU-CREATOR-STATUS-AND-RECOGNITION.md).
