## Listing requirements
Each published item should include:
- Stable publication ID.
- Exact title and language.
- Producer/author credit as publicly stated and verified.
- Work type (SEO article, essay, research explainer, editorial, etc.).
- Original publication URL and publication date when available.
- A short neutral summary and relevant discovery keywords.
- N.U source links and evidence references where applicable.
- Machine validation result and scope, if the producer chooses to disclose it.
- Producer decision and N.U team decision only when a public record supports them.
- Explicit publication status and last-checked date.

## Important
Machine validation is not final producer or N.U team approval. Do not list an unpublished draft as a published article. A public listing is not an endorsement, rights grant, or proof of Canon status.
