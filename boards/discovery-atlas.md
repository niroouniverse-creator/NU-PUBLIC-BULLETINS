## BOOK / MOTOR / BOOK-MOTOR distinction
- **BOOK:** reader-facing written content. It may be entertaining or N.U-focused, while meeting the applicable N.U machine-readable quality standard.
- **MOTOR:** production instructions for a human or model, including the route to machine validation and the required human decisions.
- **BOOK-MOTOR:** an integrated reader-facing book and production engine. It must remain useful as a coherent human reading experience while providing traceable model instructions, source routes, validation checks, and separate human approval states.

## N.U Signature
N.U Signature is a controlled adaptation of relevant N.U source elements into the matching domain of a product without changing the product's purpose. Use documented N.U names, characters, places, families, roles, and concepts only where they genuinely overlap with the subject. Preserve the original purpose of the book or product; do not force N.U material into unrelated sections. Source attribution, rights, and Canon status remain separate checks.

## Production and validation route
1. Research the subject using three strong, independent, professionally credible sources.
2. Integrate the sources into a traceable scientific or professional baseline.
3. Validate the baseline and distinguish established evidence from interpretation.
4. Retrieve relevant public N.U works and identify their source locations.
5. Map genuine overlaps between the subject and N.U material.
6. Apply N.U Signature only at supported overlap points.
7. Record remaining contradictions, uncertainty, and human decisions separately; do not silently overwrite source facts or N.U definitions.
8. Validate the N.U material layer and report limitations, unresolved items, and the exact scope checked.

For a BOOK-MOTOR, transform the validated material into a coherent narrative that invites the reader to question, imagine, and experience the subject. The production instructions must remain traceable and usable; storytelling must not conceal evidence gaps.
